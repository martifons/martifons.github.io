Same prompt.Other photo.

An egret on a turtle's shell catches a fish and feeds it to the turtle. My first version was a mess: the egret faced the wrong way and dropped the fish in the lake, even though the prompt said facing right. The video starts from the photo, and the photo wins over the text. So I fixed the photo first.

All four prompts, the ones that failed and the ones that worked. Drag the line on the video to compare version one and version two.

Soul 2 · version one

The photo that lied

It never says which way the egret looks. My first start photo. It describes the egret, the turtle and the lake well, but only says the egret's head is lowered toward the water. Soul 2 picked a direction for me: left, away from the turtle's face.

Watch out

Read the highlighted line. Without a direction, the model chooses one, and whatever it chooses is locked in for the whole video.

The prompt 130 words

Real wildlife photo taken with a long lens from the shore of a calm lake, soft overcast daylight. A great egret stands on the back of a large freshwater turtle floating in the water: the egret all white, with a long S-shaped neck, a long yellow-orange bill with a dark tip and long black legs, its feet gripping the dark olive-brown domed shell, its head lowered as it looks at the water. The turtle's head sticks out of the water in front of the shell, olive skin with thin yellow stripes, eyes open. Calm grey-green water with gentle ripples fills the picture, the bird and the turtle reflected below them, the far shore a soft blur at the top. Shallow depth of field, natural colour, sharp feather and shell detail.

Google Flow · Omni Flash · version one

Facing right. It didn't.

The prompt said facing right, twice. Same 10 seconds, 9:16, 720p, audio on, the v1 photo as reference. The prompt asks for both animals facing right. The video kept the photo's direction instead.

  • 0–10 s✕ the egret faces left the whole take
  • 2.0 s✕ it fishes behind the turtle, by its tail
  • 3.0 s✕ the fish drops in the lake
  • 6.5 s✓ it swallows the second one

Watch out

The handoff was one sentence: the egret lets the fish drop into the turtle's mouth. Omni let it drop, just into the water. One line is never enough for the moment that matters.

The prompt 941 words

FORMAT
Real wildlife video filmed with a long lens from the shore, seen as the finished exported video file: the full picture edge to edge, nothing on top of it. Whatever records the picture is never in frame. One continuous take in real time, no cuts. Vertical frame. The video runs at 24 fps. No frame is repeated. Real time, 1x speed, never slow motion.

SCENE
On a calm lake, a great egret stands on the shell of a floating turtle. It catches a small fish right in front of the turtle and lowers it into the turtle's open mouth: the turtle gulps it down. The turtle opens its mouth again for more; the egret catches a second fish, the turtle stretches up with its mouth wide open, and the egret tips its head back and swallows that one itself. The egret is already standing on the shell in the first frame. The take ends with the egret standing tall and the turtle slowly closing its mouth.

REFERENCE IMAGE
Use it for the egret, the turtle, the water, the reflections, the far shore and the light. Everything matches it exactly, zero drift, no smoothing. The movement is described below.

THE EGRET
One real great egret: all-white feathers, a long S-shaped neck, a long yellow-orange bill with a dark tip, long black legs, its feet gripping the turtle's shell the whole time. It moves like a real heron: stillness, then fast precise strikes of the neck.

THE TURTLE
One real freshwater turtle floating low in the water: a dark olive-brown domed shell, olive skin with thin yellow stripes on the head and neck. Only its head and neck move; the shell stays level, rocking slightly when the egret strikes.

THE FISH
Two small silver fish, each about the length of the egret's bill, wet and wriggling when caught. Each comes out of the water right in front of the turtle's head.

LOCATION MAP
We are on the shore 15 m away, at water level, looking at them from the side. The turtle floats in the middle of the frame facing right; the egret stands on the back half of its shell, facing right. The water fills the frame; the far shore is a soft blur at the top.

FIRST FRAME
The egret at x 15-55%, y 15-58%, standing on the shell, its head lowered toward the water in front of the turtle; the turtle's shell at x 10-55%, y 55-68%, its head out of the water at x 55-70%, y 55-62%; their reflections below at y 68-90%; calm water around them; the blurred shore across the top at y 0-6%. Instant read: a white bird standing on a turtle's back in a lake. No delayed reveal.

CAMERA
Locked off on the shore: a still frame with only the faintest natural movement of a long lens. It never pans, never zooms, never cuts, never moves closer, never goes underwater.

ACTION TIMING
0–1.5 s — The egret stands still on the shell, staring at the water just in front of the turtle's head; the turtle floats, its head up.
1.5–2.2 s — The egret's neck shoots down and its bill stabs into the water in front of the turtle with a small splash; it comes up with a small silver fish wriggling crosswise in its bill.
2.2–3.5 s — It lowers the fish to the turtle: the turtle stretches its neck up and opens its mouth wide, the egret lets the fish drop into it, and the turtle snaps its mouth shut and gulps.
3.5–5.0 s — The turtle opens its mouth again, neck stretched up, asking for more; the egret straightens its neck and looks at the water.
5.0–6.0 s — A second fast strike into the water in front of the turtle; the egret comes up with a second small fish.
6.0–7.5 s — The turtle stretches up with its mouth wide open, but the egret lifts the fish high, tips its head back and swallows it whole; a small bulge slides down its neck.
7.5–10 s — The egret stands tall on the shell and shakes a drop off its bill; the turtle slowly closes its mouth and lowers its head. Hold to the last frame.

PHYSICS AND SCALE
Real size: the egret is about 90 cm tall, the turtle's shell about 40 cm long. Each strike sends a small splash and rings of ripples across the water; the shell rocks slightly under the egret's feet and settles. Fish wriggle and drip. One egret, one turtle, two fish. Nothing morphs.

NO FULL-FRAME EVENTS
Nothing fills the frame: the egret, the turtle and the water stay in view in every frame.

LIGHTING
Soft overcast daylight, even and gentle; white feathers bright but with detail; soft reflections on the water.

AUDIO
No music, no score, no narration, no subtitles. Quiet lake sounds: soft lapping water, a sharp splash at each strike, the wet slap of the fish, a soft gulp from the turtle, distant birds. No words.

Soul 2 · version two

Fix the photo first

Both facing right, bill over the turtle. The new start photo says the direction three times and puts the egret's bill a hand's width in front of the turtle's face: the exact spot where the fish has to go, already in frame one.

Watch out

Pose the action in the photo. The shorter the distance from frame one to the moment that matters, the less the model improvises.

The prompt 140 words

Real wildlife photo taken with a long lens from the shore of a calm lake, soft overcast daylight, both animals seen from the side and facing right. A large freshwater turtle floats in the middle of the picture, its dark olive-brown domed shell just above the water, its head stretched out on the right, olive skin with thin yellow stripes. A great egret stands on the back of the shell facing right, the same way as the turtle: all white, long black legs, its long neck curved forward and down past the turtle's head, the tip of its long yellow-orange bill just above the water a hand's width in front of the turtle's face. Calm grey-green water with gentle ripples, their reflections below, the far shore a soft blur at the top. Shallow depth of field, natural colour, sharp detail.

Straight into its mouth

Ten seconds, locked off from the shore. Add the v2 photo as the reference, set 10 s, 9:16, 720p and audio on, and paste the prompt whole. The handoff is now written step by step.

  • 1.2–3.0 s✓ into the turtle's mouth
  • 3.0–5.5 s✓ more please, second strike

Rule 01

The photo beats the prompt

Which way an animal faces is decided by the first frame, not the text. If the photo is wrong, no prompt will save it. Fix the photo first.

Rule 02

Pose the action

Start with the bill already over the spot where the fish comes out. The less distance to the action, the less the model improvises.

Rule 03

Say where things end

First fish inside the turtle, never in the water. Second inside the egret. Write the handoff step by step, not in one line.
